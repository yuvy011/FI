# Visitor-Spending-in-Australia

By Utkarsh Vaishnav · FIT3179 · 35022981

A single-page visitor-spending story for the average Australian reader. Fifteen charts form three chapters of five figures: spending baskets, visitor markets, and geography/recovery.

## Sources and scope
ABS Tourism Satellite Account, ABS overseas arrivals, and TRA IVS/DoTS provide the spending and arrival series. Each chart specifies its period, units and source. Tourism-region boundaries are from TRA 2026 based on ABS ASGS 2021. Values are nominal. Full-trip expenditure, spending in Australia, arrival movements and visitor-nights are distinct measures.

## Explore the visualisation
The page follows three connected chapters:
1. What visitors buy: tourism’s domestic and international contribution, spending categories and the difference between full-trip spending and expenditure within Australia.
2. Who spends differently: comparisons by travel purpose and origin market, including education spending and visitor-arrival recovery.
3. Where & when: international spending per night across states and Queensland tourism regions, changing budget shares and tourism consumption over time.

## Data sources
- Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS): Tourism Satellite Account and Overseas Arrivals and Departures.
- Tourism Research Australia (TRA): International Visitor Survey, Domestic Tourism Statistics and tourism-region boundaries.
- Natural Earth / World Atlas: world boundaries.

Each figure includes its source, reporting period, measurement unit and a link to its readable JSON specification.
## Understanding the measures
- Domestic visitors: Australian residents travelling within Australia.
- International visitors: people living overseas who visit Australia.
- Spending per visitor-night: total expenditure divided by total visitor-nights for the same geography and reporting period.
- Budget share: category expenditure divided by the relevant total expenditure, multiplied by 100.
